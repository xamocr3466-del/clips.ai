WELCOME TO YOUR STUDIO

A shorter path to your next Short.

  1. Bring a video. Paste a public YouTube link, or upload a video from your computer.
  2. Set your style. Choose up to 5 clips, 15–60 seconds each, with fit or center crop framing.
  3. Let it work. Whisper transcribes speech on your CPU. The first run downloads a free model; longer videos need more time.
  4. Review and share. Preview every clip, check captions, and download the MP4. Upload it to YouTube yourself.
Your first successfully processed video is free. Continue with a monthly or yearly plan. You need FFmpeg, Python, disk space, and an internet connection for YouTube and the first model download. YouTube may block some links; a local upload is the fallback. Ranking suggests highlights; always review the edit.

This studio processes on the machine running the server. Processing times depend on its hardware. Use videos you own or have permission to reuse.
